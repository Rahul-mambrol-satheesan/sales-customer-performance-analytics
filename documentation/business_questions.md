# Business Questions

## Executive Performance
1. What is total revenue, profit, orders, and average order value?
2. How does revenue change month-over-month?
3. Which months have the strongest and weakest performance?
4. Which regions contribute the most revenue and profit?
5. Which sales channels have the highest average order value?

## Product Performance
6. What are the top 10 products by revenue?
7. Which product categories generate the most profit?
8. Which products sell high volume but have relatively low margins?
9. How does discounting affect profitability?
10. Which products/categories should management investigate further?

## Customer Analytics
11. How many active customers do we have?
12. What proportion of customers are repeat customers?
13. Who are the highest-value customers?
14. How does revenue differ by customer segment?
15. Which regions have the highest-value customers?

## Data Quality
16. Are order IDs unique?
17. Are there missing customer IDs?
18. Are quantities valid?
19. Are discount values within the approved range?
20. Do all customer and product IDs exist in their master tables?

## Stakeholder Deliverables
The final dashboard should allow an executive to answer:
- How are we performing?
- Where is revenue coming from?
- Which products/customers drive performance?
- Where are margins under pressure?
- What data-quality issues need attention?
